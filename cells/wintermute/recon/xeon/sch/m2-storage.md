## 5.2 SATA vs KEYM lanes
- PCH balls p.16 [visual]: PCIE9/SATA0A and PCIE10/SATA1A carry only the `PCIE_*9/10` nets to `<42>`; PCIE13/SATA0B, PCIE14/SATA1B, PCIE16/SATA3 are all X (NC); PCIE15/SATA2 carries `SATA_PRX_DTX_*2` / `SATA_PTX_DRX_*2` to `<43>`, bracket "SATA HDD" (p.16).
- So an M.2 SATA SSD in `KEYM` runs on SATA0A, the 2.5" bay on SATA2. They are separate PCH ports: NVMe + 2.5" and even M.2-SATA + 2.5" are both fine electrically (p.16, p.42, p.43).
- The Wichita guide says an M.2 SATA drive in the slot "disappears" with a second SATA drive, which contradicts p.16. Their setup used a SATA→M.2 adapter in the bay, so treat it as unverified folklore, not a board limit (p.16).
- Mechanics, not on the schematic: Dell 3520 manual says "92 WHr battery supports only SSD card and USB Type-C port, and cannot support 2.5-inch drive" (https://dell.com/support/manuals/en-us/precision-15-3520-laptop/prec3250_om_pub/removing-the-battery?guid=guid-bf6db8c1-8591-4d08-936d-8351b682bd1e&lang=en-us). Wichita claims `KEYM` and the caddy overlap on the 5580; elektroda claims both fit with caddy 06F7DD. Check on the board before buying the SATA SSD (p.43).
- Coreboot: `SataPortsEnable[0]` (KEYM SATA mode) + `[2]` (bay) + `[4]` (WWAN SATA mode); `SATAXPCIE0` is hardware-selected by `PEDET`, keep `GPP_E0` NF1 (p.16, p.42).

## WWAN slot in detail (re-check of backlog B1 against E152P)
- Lane 0, PCIE17/SATA4: RX `PCIE_PRX_DTX_P17/N17` direct; TX `PCIE_PTX_DRX_*17` → `CZ10`/`CZ11` 0.1 µF 0402 → slot. On E152P both caps are drawn **without `@`**, still inside the green "9/24 Reserve for embedded location, refer Intel PDG 0.9" box. E151P draws `@CZ10/@CZ11` [visual] (p.37).
- Lane 1, PCIE18 **or** USB3 port 2: `UZ29` PI3PCIE3212 (2nd source HD3SS3212), VDD `+3.3V_WWAN`, OE# tied low (`RZ360` 10 K GND). `B` port = USB3 port 2 (`CZ150/151` 0.1 µF on TX), `C` port = PCIE18 (`CZ152/153` 0.22 µF on TX), `A` port → slot through RF chokes `LI16`/`LI17` [visual] (p.37).
- `UZ29 SEL` = `SLOT2_CONFIG_1`: low → USB3 (WWAN module, state 8), high → PCIE18 (state 1 "SSD-PCIE (2 lane)") [visual] (p.37). The slot is wired for a **x2 PCIe SSD** (PCIE17 + PCIE18), not just x1.
- Lane 1 TX leaves the mux as `PCIE_PTX_L_DRX_*18` and reaches the slot pin as `PCIE_PTX_C_DRX_*18` only through `@RZ1`/`@RZ2` 0 Ω, drawn nopop [visual] (p.37). That link also carries USB3 TX to a modem. If it is really open, a modem only gets USB2 and an SSD only gets x1. Add it to the DMM list (same stale-BOM pattern as `RH10..17`).
- CONFIG table [visual] (p.37): state 0 SSD-SATA (all GND, `m3042_PCIE#_SATA` high), 1 SSD-PCIE 2 lane (CFG1 high), 8 WWAN (CFG0 high), 14 HCA-PCIE 1 lane (CFG0/2/3 high), 15 empty.
- `m3042_PCIE#_SATA` → PCH `GPP_F1` SATAXPCIE4 is **driven by the EC** (ball J12), `RH344` 10 K PU. The EC reads `SLOT2_CONFIG_0..3` and picks PCIe vs SATA for lane 0 [visual] (p.39, p.16).
- `WWAN_WAKE#` → EC via `@RF@RZ326`, drawn nopop [visual] (p.37).
- Changes vs backlog B1 text (E151P): USB3 port 2 goes through `UZ29`, not straight through chokes `L15/L17`; `CZ10/CZ11` not `@` on E152P; slot can do x2 PCIe; `WWAN_RADIO_DIS#` diode is `DZ5`; rail pad `PJP41` "2.5A" (p.37, p.48).
- DIY precedent: on Dell 7x80 WWAN slots the PCIe root port is disabled by BIOS and B-key PCIe cards are not detected (https://www.mattmillman.com/does-the-dell-latitude-m-2-wwan-socket-have-the-sata-interface-on-it/). On 5580/3520 there is no report of a working NVMe in the WWAN slot; the Wichita guide lists NVMe-in-WWAN only for the 5591 (https://www.thewichitacomputerguy.com/blog/how-add-second-hard-drive-dell-latitude-5580-or-5590?page=1). Assume stock BIOS keeps PCIE17/18 off; coreboot turns them on.

## WLAN slot in detail
- Key A socket; A+E 2230 cards fit. PCIe-only: the 100-series/200-series PCH has no CNVi, so CNVi cards (9560, AX201, AX211, BE201) are dead here. The CLink pins `PCH_CL_*1` are for Intel vPro PCIe cards (p.37, p.16).
- Lane 1 (PCIE1, SRC2, `CLKREQ_PCIE#2`) and the DP lanes from PS8338 `SW2_DP1` exist only for an Intel WiGig card (on E152P `SW1` goes to TB3 DPSNK1, p.29) [visual] (p.37).
- `+3.3V_WLAN` enable: both feeds `@RZ71` (`SIO_SLP_WLAN#`) and `@RZ70` (`AUX_EN_WOWL`) are drawn nopop, `RZ38` 100 K PD [visual] (p.48). One is fitted on a working board. DMM it; coreboot must drive whichever one it is (`GPD9` SLP_WLAN# or EC).
- p.35 note: "When LAN & WLAN are exist at the same time, WLAN will disable" = BIOS LAN/WLAN switching (EC/BIOS policy, not hardware) [text] (p.35).

## DMM list added by this phase
`@RZ1`/`@RZ2` (WWAN lane-1 TX), `CZ10`/`CZ11` (confirm fitted), `@RF@RZ326` (WWAN wake), `@RZ70`/`@RZ71` (WLAN power enable) (p.37, p.48).
